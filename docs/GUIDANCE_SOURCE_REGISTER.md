# Guidance source register

The prototype guidance is source-checked. Flight medical review and approved vehicle-specific procedures are pending. Source links support the medical and spaceflight context. Mission escalation language is explicitly a prototype workflow.

- Crew medical follow-up and emergency escalation context from [NASA In-Flight Medical Care](https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo/health-and-medical-systems/in-flight-medical-care/).
- Warning-sign references from the [American Heart Association](https://www.heart.org/en/about-us/heart-attack-and-stroke-symptoms) and [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/stroke/signs-symptoms/).
- Blood pressure technique reference from the [American Heart Association](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/high-blood-pressure/understanding-blood-pressure-readings/monitoring-your-blood-pressure-at-home).
- [Pulse](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003399.htm), [temperature](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003400.htm), and [glucose](https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/blood-glucose-test/) references from MedlinePlus.
- Sleep logging reference from the [NIOSH sleep diary](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/work-hour-training-for-nurses/10/sleepdiary.pdf).
