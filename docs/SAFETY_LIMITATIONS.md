# Safety limitations

SALUS is a local research prototype for synthetic data and a hackathon demonstration. Clinical validity and flight approval are unestablished. For actual mission care, use approved mission procedures and crew medical support. The app has no verified onboard procedures or integrations that automatically contact a responder. Emergency Assistance provides navigation and instructions to contact the responder.

Statistical flags describe deviation from a synthetic baseline. Real-world medical significance, treatment, and mission fitness require professional review. The broad numeric input limits check data format only. Scientific evidence supplies background. The application contains no medication or treatment protocol. Keep the instance bound to localhost and use fictional records.
